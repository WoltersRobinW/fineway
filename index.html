Questions people ask before they book.

Can you guarantee that my visa is approved?

No one honestly can. The decision is Home Affairs'. What we can do is choose the right route, make the file complete and correct, and tell you plainly if we see a risk. Our fee does not depend on the outcome.

Can I apply without leaving South Africa?

Often, yes. About half of our applications are filed in-country, as extensions or changes of status. We check in the consultation whether your route allows it.

How long is a police clearance valid?

You need one for every country you lived in for 12 months or more since turning 18, and they expire. We plan the order of your documents so none run out before the application is lodged.
